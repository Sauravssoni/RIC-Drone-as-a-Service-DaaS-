# Scheme Convergence & Public-Value Model — MVP Prototype

The proposal is intentionally **convergence-first**: use existing public investments and regulated capacity more effectively before recommending new asset procurement.

## 1. SMAM / RKVY mechanisation ecosystem

Public relevance:
- SMAM's stated objectives include increasing mechanisation reach to small/marginal farmers and low-farm-power regions and promoting Custom Hiring Centres to offset smallholding economics.
- Government updates also encourage precision agriculture tools and drones.

Proposed RAJ-KRISHI use:
- discover / onboard eligible CHC and other authorised service capacity;
- measure productive utilisation and delivered cost;
- use Coverage Contracts where ordinary market density fails;
- expose evidence that can support future policy design.

Boundary: existing asset/demonstration assistance is **not assumed to automatically finance the proposed service-fee support**. Any convergence requires Department/finance/legal approval.

## 2. NaMo Drone Didi

Public relevance:
- women SHGs are equipped and trained as agricultural drone service providers;
- programme guidelines expect State support in building adequate annual service business;
- the programme already has a Drone Portal/MIS.

Proposed RAJ-KRISHI use:
- route compatible pooled demand to eligible Drone Didi capacity;
- expose utilisation gaps and seasonal demand;
- preserve programme MIS as programme authority;
- combine Drone Didi capacity with FPO/CHC/private authorised capacity in a vendor-neutral state service layer.

Boundary: RAJ-KRISHI does not claim ownership of Drone Didi programme data or MIS.

## 3. RajKisan / Rajasthan SSO / Jan Aadhaar / e-Mitra

Public relevance:
- familiar departmental and farmer/citizen digital service journeys;
- RajKisan publicly exposes SSO login and Jan Aadhaar-assisted citizen login flows;
- Jan Aadhaar supports identity/public-service/DBT architecture for Rajasthan residents.

Proposed RAJ-KRISHI use:
- integrate as an approved service-entry / identity-handoff channel;
- minimise additional farmer onboarding;
- support assisted journeys through e-Mitra / field extension.

Boundary: evaluator SSO/Jan Aadhaar screens are `DEMO_ONLY`. No live authentication or real Aadhaar/Jan Aadhaar data is used.

## 4. AgriStack / UFSI

Proposed role:
- consent-aware FarmerID, farmland-plot and crop references;
- standardised service-event contract where authorised;
- avoid a duplicate proprietary farmer registry.

## 5. KVK + DGCA-authorised RPTO

Proposed role:
- KVK: farmer mobilisation, agronomic validation, demonstrations, extension campaigns, candidate funnel;
- RPTO: regulated pilot training/certification pathway;
- Grid: forecast exactly where/when additional pilot capacity is needed.

## 6. IMD + DGCA eGCA / Digital Sky

Proposed role:
- IMD: weather/nowcast evidence and mission-window context;
- DGCA systems: UIN/RPC/regulatory/airspace evidence under authorised workflows;
- Grid: one auditable mission state machine without pretending to become the aviation authority.

# Proposed public-value mechanisms

## A. Demand Density
Pool compatible smallholder acreage before provider dispatch.

**Goal:** reduce dead kilometres, raise productive acres per mobilisation and create a predictable work pipeline.

## B. Universal Service Coverage Contracts
For blocks where normal provider economics fail:

`coverage gap → compatible acreage bundle → eligible capacity call → Department-configured support → verified service → payment evidence`

This is a proposed operational mechanism, not a claim that Rajasthan currently runs such a drone support scheme.

## C. Proof-of-Service
Make `verified acre-service` the measurable public-value unit.

Possible policy outputs:
- ₹ / verified acre-service;
- turnaround time;
- small/marginal share;
- tribal/remote coverage;
- provider utilisation;
- service exceptions / grievances;
- mission evidence completeness.

## D. Seasonal Capacity Planning
Use crop calendars, demand and productive-capacity factors to decide whether the next intervention should be:
- training;
- fleet mobilisation;
- battery staging;
- maintenance capacity;
- transport hub;
- additional provider empanelment.

This reduces the risk of treating “buy more drones” as the default answer.
