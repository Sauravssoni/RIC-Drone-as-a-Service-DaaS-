# Commercial & Scale Model — MVP Prototype

## Commercial principle

RAJ-KRISHI DRONE GRID is proposed as **Government / institutional infrastructure**, not a consumer subscription app.

### Explicit exclusions
- no sale of farmer personal data;
- no advertising model based on farmer profiles;
- no mandatory farmer subscription;
- no proprietary farmer identity master;
- no requirement that Government buy Syntheon hardware for the central platform to work.

## Proposed revenue lines

### 1. Implementation & Integration — one-time
Covers:
- Department configuration;
- GIS / district-block service model;
- approved Government adapters;
- provider/RPC onboarding workflows;
- policy and agronomic rule-pack implementation;
- security, privacy, UAT and migration;
- training and production handover.

### 2. Managed Platform / O&M — annual
Covers:
- hosting / observability;
- SLA support;
- security patching and periodic assessment;
- integration maintenance;
- analytics / dashboards;
- user / provider support;
- training refresh;
- versioned policy/rule changes.

### 3. Verified-Service Orchestration — optional procurement structure
Where Government chooses a usage-linked commercial structure, a transparent fee can be tied to **verified** service volume / SLA rather than app users or hidden data monetisation.

Any such model is subject to procurement policy, price discovery, applicable taxes and Government approval.

### 4. Optional SUTRA field edge / enablement
Where weak connectivity, assisted service or evidence quality justifies it:
- field node supply / lease;
- device management;
- support / replacement;
- field workflow customisation.

SUTRA remains optional.

### 5. State-to-state deployment / institutional licensing
The orchestration logic can be configured for other states around their own registries, SSO, DBT and agriculture portals using interoperable contracts. This is **not** a proposal to create one proprietary national farmer database.

# Indicative 90-day Rajasthan pilot

**Total proposed pilot:** ₹74.80 lakh  
**Core drone procurement:** ₹0

The pilot prioritises the software, integration, evidence and operating-model gap before recommending additional asset procurement.

Indicative cost buckets:
1. platform / GIS / service orchestration;
2. Demand Density + Universal Coverage engines;
3. Government adapter contracts / approved integration work;
4. compliance + agronomic rule packs;
5. provider / officer / field journeys;
6. proof-of-service / audit / reconciliation;
7. security / privacy / observability;
8. pilot operations / UAT / training / PMO / handover;
9. measurement and state-scale design.

Final line-item budget remains subject to Department scope and procurement norms.

# Economic measurement framework

The pilot should establish a baseline rather than assume benefits.

## Farmer economics
- delivered ₹ / acre-service;
- booking/request-to-service time;
- assisted-access effort;
- repeat-visit / failed-service rate;
- grievance closure.

## Provider economics
- productive acres / operating day;
- dead kilometres / mobilisation;
- utilisation rate;
- settlement cycle;
- cancellation / weather loss;
- battery / logistics downtime.

## Government economics
- ₹ support / verified acre-service;
- incremental remote/smallholder reach;
- evidence exception rate;
- cost of provider-capacity gap closure;
- utilisation of publicly supported assets.

## Extension economics
- signal-to-scout time;
- scout-to-decision time;
- cost / acre assessed;
- follow-up completion;
- avoided blanket deployment where scouting does not justify action.

# Scale roadmap

## 0–3 months — Controlled DaaS MVP pilot
Prove the service and evidence loop.

## 3–12 months — Rajasthan Drone Mission Exchange
Multi-provider service orchestration, universal coverage and Department campaigns.

## 12–24 months — Agri-Extension Digital Twin
Combine official signals / optional FarmGraph with targeted scouting, human decision and outcome verification.

## 24–36 months — Agricultural Mechanisation Service Grid
Subject to policy, selectively extend the verified physical-service model to CHC machinery, sensing, mapping, soil/testing and other mechanisation services.

## 2030 vision — Interoperable Bharat Agri Mission Grid
Reusable state architectures around national/state DPI and UFSI-style contracts, preserving local authority and avoiding a proprietary national farmer master.
